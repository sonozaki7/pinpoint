01 How it works

Seven steps. Point, say, send.

Silence means agree. You only speak when something is wrong, and every note stays attached to the exact sentence it answers.

  1. 01

    Split

    Your agent’s plan becomes one line per step. Each line is tied to a verbatim quote from the original message, so nothing is invented.

    quote-verified · 4–10 points

  2. 02

    Point

    Ring to the step. Tap and speak one sentence; silence agrees. Your note stays on that exact line, and Pinpoint moves you to the next line that needs you.

    R1 · tap / double-tap / hold

  3. 03

    Send

    Tap Send for a five-second undo countdown. Pinpoint composes a cited review — objections first, answers, notes, then the points you agreed to — and the agent keeps working.

    5-second undo · deterministic composer

Silence means agree. Your correction is the signal.

05 Privacy & safety

A microphone that is on only when you are.

In this simulator

  • Sample threads and notes live in this browser tab. Closing it clears them; a random device ID is kept in local storage for voice rate limits.
  • Audio is captured only between the tap that starts recording and the tap that stops it.
  • A voice clip goes to our Whisper server for transcription; the audio is not kept with your review.
  • If the mic is blocked or the server is unreachable, suggestions and a text box take over. Review never stalls.

On the real device

  • The glasses record only after you tap, and show a recording marker the whole time.
  • Pinpoint Cloud gives each device a private sample workspace, deleted after seven days without use.
  • Pairing a desk is opt-in. Without pairing, nothing reaches your own computer.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no account needed for the sample workspace.
