How to use

  • Scroll to move between threads and points.
  • Tap to open a thread, talk about a point, or choose an answer.
  • Double-tap to go back, send a review, or exit from the inbox.
  • Hold to open the menu to refresh, reset the sample, or read on your phone.

Workspace

Reset is available in the sample workspace.

Privacy

Pinpoint sends your review notes and short voice clips to the Pinpoint server so your agent can act on them. Voice is transcribed, then discarded. A paired computer's requests are relayed through Pinpoint Cloud without being stored. The sample workspace is private to this device and deleted after 7 days without use.
